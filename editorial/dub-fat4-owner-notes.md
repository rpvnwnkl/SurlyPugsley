# DUB FAT4 owner notes — September 13, 2026

Draft source notes for the crank reference page; not yet published site copy.

## Owner account, lightly edited

My spinning and knee pain improved with the 170 mm cranks. Fatigue over long rides too, I guess. That’s one thing I noticed this winter on the Moonie: not a great cruising machine.

Installation was by the book and seems to work fine.

Going 1× with DUB cranks makes the bike feel lightweight and look great. Rings are easy to change, but the range of ring sizes with the offset I wanted was limited. Mine has the three-bolt pattern, so a spider is something I could explore. I haven’t tried that yet, but I could see gravel fatties trying it.

## Editorial distinctions

- Comfort and fatigue changes are the owner’s experience, not a general medical claim. No weight measurements were supplied.
- The earlier account identifies tested Shimano 12-speed shifting with a SRAM cassette and chain. “Put whatever drivetrain in back” should not become a universal compatibility statement.
- Spider use is an untested idea. No specific spider, ring combination, chainline, or clearance has been verified.
- Exact installed spacers were not recalled; the owner reports following the manufacturer instructions. Do not reconstruct them from memory.
- SRAM references are in docs/archive/sram-documents. The illustrated digital installation manual is now preserved offline with local image assets.
