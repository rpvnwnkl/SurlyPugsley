Pugsley Frame¶

The Pugsley frame is designed for extreme conditions and versatility, featuring:

  • 4130 CroMoly steel construction
  • 135mm rear spacing (for use with standard MTB hubs)
  • Clearance for super-wide tires
  • Unique offset design for drivetrain and wheel interchangeability
  • Disc and rim brake compatibility

Fork¶

  • 4130 CroMoly steel
  • 135mm hub spacing (matches rear)
  • Disc and rim brake mounts
  • Eyelets for racks and fenders

Frame Details¶

The Pugsley’s unique frame and fork design allow for: - Swapping front and rear wheels in the field - Maximum reliability in remote conditions - Use of standard MTB drivetrain parts

For full geometry, sizing, and technical details, see the archived original page.
