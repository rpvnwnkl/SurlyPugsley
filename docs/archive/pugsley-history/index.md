# Early Pugsley pages and technical articles

Explore how Surly presented the early Pugsley and Neck Romancer, and read its explanations of the design and hub compatibility. **Publisher: Surly Bikes.** These are selected sources, not a complete year-by-year collection.

Each reading copy links to its original source HTML and the archived page. Pictures may come from nearby capture dates, as recorded in the collection metadata.

## Surly technical articles

These are captures of the current Surly website, collected September 29, 2026 UTC. Their original publication and revision dates have not been verified. They discuss historical designs; match the described equipment to your bike.

- [Pugsley design explanation](reference-4/reading.html): tire clearance, drivetrain offset and interchangeable wheels. [Original article](https://surlybikes.com/pages/pugsley).
- [Hub, fork and disc-brake compatibility](reference-5/reading.html): how Surly distinguished its offset and non-offset Pugsley fork arrangements. [Original article](https://surlybikes.com/pages/hub-fork-and-disc-brake-compatibility).
