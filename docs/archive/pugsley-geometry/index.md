[Download the preservation bundle](surly-pugsley-preservation.zip). It includes the original source HTML, styled reading copies and assets, reference screenshots, provenance and verification records.

The preserved pages retain Surly’s appearance, with specifications expanded for reading. They are local visual copies, not operating storefronts: scripts are preserved as files but disabled in the reading copies, and checkout, search and account services do not work offline. External navigation links lead to the original website.

These are present-day captures of Surly’s legacy references. They are not dated Internet Archive snapshots and do not prove that every earlier model year had the same specifications.
