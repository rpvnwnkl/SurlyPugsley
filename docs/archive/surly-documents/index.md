# Surly catalogs and crank documents

A growing collection of original Surly documents for looking up Pugsley frames, builds, and parts. These are complete local copies, so the reference remains available if the original links move.

## Catalogs by year

Catalog specifications describe the builds and parts listed for that year. They do not necessarily identify what is fitted to a used bike today.

| Catalog | Useful for |
| --- | --- |
| [2005 Surly catalog](surly-2005-catalog.pdf) | The original Pugsley frameset and design specifications. |
| [2008 Surly catalog](surly-2008-catalog.pdf) | Early Mr. Whirly crank options. |
| [2010 Surly catalog](surly-2010-catalog.pdf) | Pugsley and crank reference before the 2011 catalog. |
| [2011 Surly catalog](surly-2011-catalog.pdf) | Complete Pugsley specifications and Mr. Whirly options. |
| [2012 Surly catalog](surly-2012-catalog.pdf) | Pugsley, Moonlander, and the MWOD system. |
| [2014 Surly catalog](surly-2014-catalog.pdf) | Special Ops-era bikes and crank components. |
| [2015 Surly catalog](surly-2015-catalog.pdf) | Comparing later Pugsley builds and parts. |

## Cranks and chainrings

- [Mr. Whirly instructions](mr-whirly-instructions.pdf) — manufacturer instructions for the crank system.
- [MWOD chainring instructions](mwod-instructions.pdf) — manufacturer instructions for the Mr. Whirly Offset Double chainring arrangement.

The links open the full PDFs for reading or downloading.

## Where these copies came from

Published by Surly Bikes. Copies retrieved September 13, 2026 from Surly’s official [catalog page](https://surlybikes.com/pages/catalogs) and [instructions page](https://surlybikes.com/pages/instructions). Documents retain their original contents; descriptions on this page are our own finding aids. Instruction revision dates have not yet been cataloged.
