# Sources and archive

## Dave Gray interview

[Read the preserved interview](interview_with_dave_gray_from_surly_bikes/article.md) with Pugsley designer Dave Gray, published by Fat-Bike.com in May 2015. His answers describe the original design and development. The article also includes interviewer commentary and humor; it is not a service manual or a specification for all model years.

The repository retains the [HTML copy](interview_with_dave_gray_from_surly_bikes/article.html), Markdown copy and associated images unchanged. [Original publication](https://fat-bike.com/2015/05/interview-with-dave-gray-from-surly-bikes/).
