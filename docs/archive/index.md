# Sources and archive

Find the documents behind the Pugsley's design, dimensions, and components. Start with a question, or browse the collections below.

## What are you looking for?

- **Why was the Pugsley offset?** Start with [the original design](../pugsley/overview.md), then read [designer Dave Gray's interview](interview_with_dave_gray_from_surly_bikes/article.md).
- **What frame dimensions did Surly publish?** Compare the [2016 legacy and Gen. 2 geometry tables](../frame/geometry.md), with links to the manufacturer references.
- **What did a particular catalog list?** Browse the [selected Surly catalogs](surly-documents/index.md#catalogs-by-year). The collection has seven catalogs spanning 2005–2015, with gaps between years.
- **Where are the crank instructions?** Find [Mr. Whirly and MWOD instructions](surly-documents/index.md#cranks-and-chainrings), or the [SRAM DUB references](sram-documents/index.md). Match the document to the crank and bottom bracket on your bike.
- **What did Surly's product pages look like?** Explore the preserved pages listed below, including a 2010 Internet Archive capture.

## Surly catalogs and crank documents

[Browse the Surly collection](surly-documents/index.md) for catalog specifications and Mr. Whirly and MWOD instructions. Catalogs describe what Surly listed at the time; the parts on a used bike may differ.

## SRAM DUB references

[Open the SRAM DUB collection](sram-documents/index.md) for installation instructions, an illustrated offline manual, and the archived compatibility chart. These references cover the labeled crank and bottom-bracket variants; they are not a general Pugsley fit approval.

## Dave Gray interview

[Read the interview](interview_with_dave_gray_from_surly_bikes/article.md), published by Fat-Bike.com in May 2015, for the designer's account of the original Pugsley's development. The interview also contains commentary and humor; its scope is the design story, rather than a model-year specification list.

## Preserved Surly product pages

[Open the 2016 legacy and Gen. 2 references](pugsley-geometry/index.md) for Surly's product descriptions, geometry, and linked frame sheets. These are present-day captures of legacy references; their capture dates do not establish earlier model-year specifications.

[Browse the early Pugsley archive](pugsley-history/index.md) for frame-page captures from 2009–2011, the 2012 Neck Romancer page, and Surly's design and hub-compatibility articles.

[Open the restored April 2010 frame page](pugsley-frame-2010/offline.html) to see its original background artwork and navigation graphics. The reconstruction combines assets from nearby captures; four full-size gallery images remain unavailable, with thumbnails preserved. The archive guide records the remaining gaps and provides original source files and collection downloads.
