## Highest priority findings

- **Unsupported modification advice:** electric.md says “Requires dropouts to be heat-treated”; upgrades.md recommends “SRAM DUB 83 mm” after describing a 100 mm shell. No conversion instructions or validation support these recommendations. Remove both guides.
- **Contradicted original hub story:** buying-guide.md, fork.md and hubs.md invent a 63 mm generation. The local Dave Gray interview describes the original 135 mm hub system and 17.5 mm offset. Remove these guides.
- **Contradicted headset advice:** headset.md recommends a tapered setup for 2011–2017. Surly’s 2016 page specifies a 1-1/8 inch headset/steerer. Remove the compatibility guide, not just the date label.
- **Overgeneralized wheel compatibility:** the local interview explains the original offset fork. Surly’s 2016 page describes a symmetric fork and an optional offset fork for wheel swapping. Do not generalize the original design to all bikes.
- **Frame reliability assertions:** reinforcements.md supplies dimensions, serial-number rules and inspection intervals without primary support; its two photos do not exist. Remove.

## Other material findings

History contains mismatched citation labels (including a browser-related Reddit link attached to a frame claim), unverified quotations and a sentence cut off mid-URL. It also declares discontinuation before describing a later model. Surly’s Gen. 2 specifications do support 142 mm rear spacing with a 135 mm adapter option; that part must not be labeled fabricated merely because other history is unreliable. Its “Gnot Boost” wording and universal generation claims are not supported by that specification.

Geometry contains “XX mm”; FAQ contains “A: Answer”; manuals links to example.com. Rim, tire, hub, drivetrain and luggage shopping lists lack adequate supporting documentation. Unsupported does not mean every entry has been proved false. Remove these lists instead of manufacturing replacements or treating embedded URLs as verified citations.
