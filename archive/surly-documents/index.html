Surly catalogs and crank documents¶

A growing collection of original Surly documents for looking up Pugsley frames, builds, and parts. These are complete local copies, so the reference remains available if the original links move.

Catalogs by year¶

Catalog specifications describe the builds and parts listed for that year. They do not necessarily identify what is fitted to a used bike today.

Catalog Useful for
2005 Surly catalog The original Pugsley frameset and design specifications.
2008 Surly catalog Early Mr. Whirly crank options.
2010 Surly catalog Pugsley and crank reference before the 2011 catalog.
2011 Surly catalog Complete Pugsley specifications and Mr. Whirly options.
2012 Surly catalog Pugsley, Moonlander, and the MWOD system.
2014 Surly catalog Special Ops-era bikes and crank components.
2015 Surly catalog Comparing later Pugsley builds and parts.

Cranks and chainrings¶

Where these copies came from¶

Published by Surly Bikes. Copies retrieved September 13, 2026 from Surly’s official catalog page and instructions page. Documents retain their original contents; descriptions on this page are our own finding aids. Instruction revision dates have not yet been cataloged.
