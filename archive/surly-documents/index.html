Surly catalogs and crank documents¶

Find published Pugsley specifications and crank instructions in complete local copies of Surly documents.

Publisher: Surly Bikes. Catalog editions held: 2005–2015, selected years listed below. Retrieved: September 13, 2026. The instruction revision dates have not yet been verified; the catalog year is not the capture date.

Catalogs by year¶

Catalog specifications describe the builds and parts listed for that year. They do not necessarily identify what is fitted to a used bike today.

The links below open the full catalog at a useful starting point. PDF page counts from the first page of the file; printed numbers refer to the catalog itself. Some PDFs contain two printed pages per screen. If your viewer ignores the link's page target, enter the PDF page number manually.

Collection coverage¶

These are selected catalogs, not a complete annual run. We hold 2005, 2008, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2014, and 2015. Within that span, 2006, 2007, 2009, and 2013 are not held here. No catalogs outside that span are currently in this collection. A gap means we lack a copy, not that Surly necessarily published a catalog for that year.
